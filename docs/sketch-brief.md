# Sketches for TJ’s portfolio

Draw these separately so each can be positioned and made clickable:

- **Work:** a wonky open laptop, with `</>` or a tiny window on the screen. Write “My work” underneath.
- **About:** a small self-portrait, smiley, or your own personal symbol. Write “Who’s this?” beside it.
- **Contact:** an open envelope or a paper airplane. Write “Say hello”.
- **Loose marks:** two arrows, one messy star, a scribbled circle, and a short patch of crosshatching. These can overlap the photo and point toward links.
- **Optional personal piece:** a golf ball, camera, shoe, or something else you actually care about. This is decoration until it has a destination.

Use black ink on white paper, or a transparent digital canvas. Cobalt blue is the accent color. Keep the imperfect strokes; avoid cleaning everything into perfect geometric shapes. Leave generous blank space around every drawing and all lettering so nothing gets clipped. Export each drawing separately as a PNG, ideally at least 1000 px wide. A straight-on photo or scan of paper works too.

The homepage now uses the supplied laptop, illustrated portrait, and Contact Me postal sticker. The contact page retains an initial SVG envelope. The signature and portrait are TJ’s supplied originals. Home is a collage; Work, About, and Contact are separate routes. Project pages contain the selected work and supplied contribution stories.
